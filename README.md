🏁 1. Project Overview
This project analyzes the performance of the top five Formula 1 teams during the 2008 season, focusing on how their results changed from race to race.
The goal is to understand consistency, improvement, and overall competitiveness across the full season.

🎯 2. Business Question
How did the top five Formula 1 teams perform during the 2008 season, and how did their performance evolve across all 18 races?

This question helps reveal not just final standings, but the full performance journey of each team.

💡 3. Hypothesis
Teams that score points consistently will show a stable or improving trend throughout the season.

🗂️ 4. Dataset
The project uses three main tables:

Constructor Standings — cumulative points and positions

Constructors — team information

Races — race calendar and round numbers

All data was filtered to the 2008 season for accuracy and clarity.

🧮 5. Measures Created
To summarize performance, three key measures were built:

Total Points — total points scored across the season

Average Position — average finishing position

Improvement Score — compares early‑season vs late‑season performance

These measures highlight consistency, competitiveness, and improvement.

📊 6. Dashboard Overview
The Power BI dashboard includes:

Race Table — cumulative points and positions per race

Line Chart — performance trend across all 18 rounds

Improvement Score Bar Chart — shows which teams improved or declined

KPI Cards — quick summary of total points and average position

Together, these visuals provide a complete picture of the season.

🔍 7. Key Insights
Ferrari
Low, stable line → strong and consistent performance

McLaren
Very close to Ferrari → competitive in almost every race

BMW Sauber
Strong start → slight decline in second half

Red Bull
Weak start → steady improvement

Renault
Midfield early → strong improvement later

These patterns show how each team developed throughout the year.

This project provides a clear view of how the top five teams performed during the 2008 Formula 1 season.

This analysis reveals not just the final results, but the full story of each team.
